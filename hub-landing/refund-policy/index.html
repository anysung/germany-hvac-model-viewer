Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-22

This policy explains what happens when you cancel your HeatPump DataBase (Europe) subscription, and when a refund is or is not due. Paddle is our merchant of record and handles all payments.

During the free trial

Every subscription begins with a 7-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. Cancel before the trial ends and no payment is taken.

After the trial

If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the first payment is taken and the paid period begins.

14-day refund on your first payment

If your first payment is taken after the free trial, you may request a full refund of that first payment within 14 days of the charge, for any reason. Send a New inquiry from the Account page or email support@heatpumpdb.eu, and we will process it with Paddle. This 14-day right applies to the first payment only; later renewals are governed by the sections below.

Cancelling a paid subscription

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal — it does not end the current period.

Access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Unused time

We do not automatically refund unused time on a period that has already been paid for.

How to cancel

Cancel through Manage billing on the Account page.

Deleting your HeatPump DataBase account does NOT cancel your subscription. Billing is handled separately and must be cancelled through Manage billing.

Exceptional refunds

If you believe your case is exceptional (for example a duplicate charge), send a New inquiry from the Account page or email support@heatpumpdb.eu, and we will review it with Paddle.

Merchant of record

Subscription payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, acting as merchant of record for all subscriptions. Invoices and VAT receipts are issued by Paddle. Refund and cancellation requests can be sent to support@heatpumpdb.eu.
