# 2. Full Version — Extended Featured Article

## Europe’s Heating Transition: Five Markets, Five Realities

### HeatPump DataBase Europe launches its new monthly Special Report series, combining verified market data, policy developments and the latest 2026 heat-pump signals across Europe’s major heating markets.

**HeatPump DataBase Europe Editorial Team | August 2026**

Europe’s heating transition is accelerating, but the pace, technology mix and policy environment remain markedly different from one country to another.

The inaugural **HeatPump DataBase Europe Special Report — “Europe’s Heating Transition: Five Markets, Five Realities”** examines the latest available market evidence from **Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Poland and Italy**, combining 2025 full-year market data with the newest available 2026 indicators through August.

At the European level, heat-pump sales returned to growth in 2025, with approximately **2.9 million units sold across 21 European countries** and an installed stock of around **29.3 million units**. The national picture, however, remains far more complex.

- **Germany:** Heat pumps became the country’s largest individual heating-technology category in 2025, while H1 2026 sales reached **194,500 units, up 40% year on year**.
- **United Kingdom:** Hydronic heat-pump sales continued to rise, but boilers still dominate the heating market and the gap between product sales and actual retrofit deployment remains significant.
- **France:** The hydronic heating market has contracted substantially since 2021, while the apparent heat-pump share increasingly reflects a shrinking overall market rather than rapid growth in absolute heat-pump volume.
- **Poland:** The market is showing renewed momentum in 2026, with **H1 heating heat-pump sell-in up 18%**, although inventory effects and differences in market definitions remain essential to understanding the data.
- **Italy:** Boiler demand declined sharply in 2025, while the latest H1 2026 data show **air-to-water heat-pump volumes up 25.5%**, signalling stronger momentum in electrified heating solutions.

## A New Monthly Market-Intelligence Series

Beginning with this edition, **HeatPump DataBase Europe plans to publish a new European Heat Pump Special Report every month**, focusing on the market developments most relevant to installers, manufacturers, distributors, architects, consultants and other heating-industry professionals.

Future editions will track not only unit sales, but also **policy changes, subsidy programmes, market structure, technology shifts, channel behaviour and emerging 2026–2027 trends** across major European markets.

The objective is to provide a clearer view of what is actually happening behind headline market-share figures — particularly where national definitions, product scopes and sales-versus-installation statistics differ.

## More Than a Static PDF

The Special Report has been developed as an **interactive digital report rather than a conventional static document**.

Readers can move through the report page by page like a digital magazine and interact directly with the visual data. **Moving the cursor over charts and individual data points reveals the underlying numerical values**, making it possible to examine market changes in greater detail without overcrowding each page with labels.

The report also includes direct source references, national market chapters, 2026 market signals and methodology notes designed to make the underlying evidence transparent and easier to verify.
