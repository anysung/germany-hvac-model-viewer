## 1. Final-report editorial rules

The final v9 report was built around the following rules. They are part of the research record and should travel with any future derivative article, presentation, infographic, or country edition.

1. **Sales are not installations.** Manufacturer / trade sell-in data remain sales unless the publishing source explicitly reports installations or deployments.
2. **Scope determines the number.** Air-to-air, hydronic heat pumps, domestic hot-water heat pumps, hybrids, boilers, and other heating generators are not counted consistently across countries.
3. **National shares are not a cross-country ranking.** Germany, the UK, France, Poland, and Italy use incompatible denominators and product scopes.
4. **Derived values are identified as HeatPump DB calculations.** Where Uniclima, EHI/HPA, or another source publishes components but no consolidated total/share, arithmetic is labelled derived.
5. **Missing evidence is not filled by estimation.** If no defensible public figure exists, the report uses `NOT FOUND` / `not published` rather than manufacturing precision.
6. **Latest 2026 signals are shown separately from full-year 2025 series.** Part-year 2026 results are never silently appended to annual charts as if they were full-year data.
7. **Charts are evidence aids, not the report itself.** The narrative interpretation explains denominator effects, inventory distortion, policy changes, and differences between sales and deployment.

**Three rules that governed the research, and must govern anything written from it:**

   interpolated or reconstructed from percentage changes. Where a figure could not be verified

### 2.6 Contradictions and traps — Germany

1. **BWP revised its own 2026 forecast down ~19 %.** At FORUM Wärmepumpe (28.11.2025) BWP raised
   BAU 2026 to **410,000** (KLIMA scenario >530,000). The *Branchenstudie 2026* (30.03.2026) puts
   BAU 2026 at **332,500**, reaching ~403,000 only in the ambitious ZIEL scenario.
   **Do not print 410,000 as BWP's current forecast** — trade blogs still circulate it.
2. **2025 total: 616,000 vs 627,000.** The interim BDH publication (Jan–Oct 2025, 24.11.2025)
   projected ~616,000 (−14 %). The final figure is **627,000 (−12 %)**. Use 627,000 only.
3. **H1 2026 heat pumps: 194,500 (BDH PDF) vs 195,000 (BWP news).** Same statistic, different
   rounding. Prefer 194,500 if the 352,000 total is shown, so the share divides.
4. **2025 share 47.7 % vs "48 %".** BWP tabulates 48 %; 299,000/627,000 = 47.7 %. Both defensible.
   Safest phrasing: "nearly half", with the units footnoted.
5. **Scope mismatch risk.** BDH "Wärmeerzeuger" excludes air/air and DHW heat pumps. IEA/EHPA-style
   European figures include them and will not reconcile with 299,000. Never mix series in one chart.
6. **2024 was not a normal decline.** −46 % vs 2023 for both total and heat pumps, and 2023 was
   itself an anomalous pull-forward peak (1,308,500). A 2021→2025 CAGR line misleads; show bars.

   pump momentum.

## 8. Suggested angles for the monthly European special report

Grounded in the data above — each is supportable with what is in this file:

1. **"Europe is not one heat pump market, it is five."** The share ranges from 7 % (UK) to 48 %
   (Germany) — and a good part of that spread is *definition*, not physics. A report that explains
   the measurement problem is more useful to installers than one that ranks countries.
2. **The denominator story.** France and Germany both show rising heat pump share with falling or
   flat heat pump volumes; the boiler market is shrinking faster than heat pumps are growing.
   Share is not momentum.
3. **The 2023 bubble and its unwind.** Germany 1,308,500 → 712,500; Poland 249,760 → 158,480;
   Italy hybrids 124,731 → 29,467. Three countries, one pattern: pull-forward, then destocking.
4. **Subsidy dependence, measured.** Poland: only ~1 in 5 heat pumps sold in 2025 carried a
   subsidy. Germany: 288,000 KfW commitments against 299,000 units. The contrast is the story.
5. **Targets vs run-rate.** UK needs 33 % compound sales growth to reach 450,000 by 2030 while
   Q1 2026 retrofit fell 22 %. Germany needs to roughly double to reach 600,000/year. France's
   "1 million" is a production target being read as an installation one.
6. **What no one publishes.** Not a single one of the five markets publishes an installation
   census for boilers. Every "installations" number in the trade press is a sales number wearing a
   different word. That is a genuinely original observation for a database company to make.
