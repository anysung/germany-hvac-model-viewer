# Installation videos — admission policy (owner, 2026-09-03)

Three tiers. The tier is stored on every entry and decides where and how it
renders. Nothing is ever listed without passing its tier's gate.

## Tier A — manufacturer official, market language
The manufacturer's own channel (or its national subsidiary's), speaking the
market's language. The default and the top of every page.

## Tier B — manufacturer official, English
Same channels, English audio, shown on every market ("English videos — all
markets"). Subtitles are labelled from DETECTED caption tracks only: a
creator-provided track earns a CC badge; YouTube auto-translate earns nothing.
The two are never conflated (house rule since day one).

## Tier C — qualified third parties, disclaimer-labelled
Owner decision 2026-09-03: official channels alone cannot cover the market
leaders (Bosch DE, Viessmann DE, Saunier Duval, Ariston and Samsung publish
no install films at all), so qualified third-party videos are admitted —
**under the conditions below, never silently.**

Eligible source classes, in order of preference:
  1. industry associations and accredited training academies
  2. trade media (e.g. SHK-TV)
  3. identifiable professional installers — real company, imprint/site
     traceable, full professional install shown

Per-video admission gate (recorded in the store as `review`):
  - channel identity resolved to a real organisation
  - title/description consistent with professional work; no DIY framing,
    no anonymous uploader, no refrigerant work presented as an owner task
  - embeddable (oEmbed 200) and upload year recorded — the refrigerant
    transition makes era visible and it stays visible on the card
  - content spot-check is an EDITORIAL act: the reviewer notes what was
    checked; a video no one has opened is not admitted

Rendering rules (enforced by InstallPage):
  - Tier C renders in its OWN section, below every official section, with a
    grey "third-party" badge on each card and the tier disclaimer repeated
    at the section head — it must never blend into the official rows
  - removal on request from the rightsholder or the featured company is the
    default answer, no debate (same posture as news source attribution)

## What never gets in, at any tier
Anonymous DIY builds, homeowner vlogs, videos whose point is lead
generation rather than the work, and anything presenting refrigerant-circuit
work as uncertified-friendly.

## Standing disclaimers (all five languages, never trimmed)
Every page: the exact model's installation manual and local regulations
outrank any video; refrigerant work is for certified personnel only.
Tier C additionally: "Third-party production — not manufacturer material;
listing is neither verification nor endorsement."
